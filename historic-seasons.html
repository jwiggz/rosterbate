Mode Stack
What This Lane Becomes
The goal is not one nostalgia gimmick. The goal is a reusable archive layer where every season can support three different products at once.
Draft The Era Open the full player pool from that year, build a custom roster, and turn the pack into your own basketball universe.
Sim Season Run the same pack through the box-score simulation layer so every reveal is generated from ratings and lineup decisions instead of sourced stats.
Reimagined Season Branch the era into a new alternate timeline before the season unfolds, then let the reveal loop tell the new story forward.
Replay Era Rosters Keep the real roster identities intact as an authenticity lane, but treat it as the supporting mode beside the custom-universe fantasy.
